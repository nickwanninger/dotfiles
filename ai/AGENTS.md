Evidence before code, always.
Push back on major sweeping changes. They should be thought through deeply and motivated.
Apply a practical subset of the ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English standard to all responses, documentation, comments, plans, and commit messages. 


<vocab>
- **One Meaning per Word:** Use the same approved term consistently for an action (e.g., use "Make sure" or "Verify", but do not alternate between verify, check, and confirm).
- **Simple Tenses Only:** Use simple present, simple past, or simple future tenses. Avoid complex verb constructions and auxiliary verbs (e.g., use "We received", not "We have been receiving").
- **Limit Multi-Word Nouns:** Do not write noun clusters or multi-word nouns that have more than three words.
- **Ban Conversational Bloat:** Do not include preambles, recaps, pleasantries, or empty filler text. Eliminate marketing words like *seamless, robust, powerful, effortless, evolutionary,* or *blast radius*.
</vocab>
